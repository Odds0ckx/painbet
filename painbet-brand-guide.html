The idea

Pain.bet reads as a hospital, not a casino. Admissions, diagnosis, a pain scale, a chart, discharge. Cold, clinical, slightly wrong, and it reads as more expensive for it. Every instinct that says “make it fun” is the wrong instinct here. Restraint is what reads as money.

Positioning

Where a casino brand performs excitement, pain.bet performs procedure. The player is a patient. A win is a diagnosis, not a celebration. The tone stays flat even when the numbers are big.

01

Clinical

Hospital intake, not casino floor. Charts, scales, wards, admissions. Instrument, not confetti.

02

Deadpan

Facts stated flat. “Ready.” “Nothing today.” No exclamation marks, ever.

03

Restrained

Four colors, two type families, one motif. Nothing extra fighting for attention.

HOW PAIN.BET TALKS
Flat, factual, never excited.

Rules

  • Short, declarative sentences. State the fact, stop.
  • No exclamation marks. Not on a win, not on an error.
  • No em dashes, ever. Use a comma, a period, or parentheses.
  • No casino language. A win is a result, not an event.
  • Never invent a promise. Unclear terms stay unclear, not filled with a placeholder.

Banned words

luckybonanza megafortune congratulationsyou won!

Voice in practice

"Ready."
"Nothing today. Back tomorrow. The wheel resets at midnight UTC."
"Paid to your wallet on verification."
"The whole jackpot. Paid on verification."
"Issued once. There is no second opinion."

MOTION & MOTIFS
Restraint over celebration.

Standard ease

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)

Every UI transition, 300ms. Fast start, gentle settle. Never a linear or bouncy curve.

Reveals & spins

easeOutCubic, full speed at t=0

Large reveals start at full speed and ease only into the stop. Never ease in from zero: a slow start reads as the interface doing nothing.

On a win

The result changes color and weight. Nothing else. No confetti, no particle bursts, no screen shake, no hard white flashes, ever.

The triangle is the one motif allowed to stand alone: hub caps, bezel
marks, pointers, corner accents. Grain and noise belong in imagery, never in UI chrome.
