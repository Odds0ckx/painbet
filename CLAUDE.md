# Repo-wide standing instructions

- **Always send a screenshot and wait for explicit go-ahead before committing any visual/UI change.** This applies to every file in this repo (`index.html`, `index-v2.html`, `painbet-*.html`, etc.), not just one workstream. Don't `git commit`/push a CSS/HTML/visual change until the user has seen a screenshot and said to proceed.
- **When redoing or restyling any page, bring it onto the index-v2 console chrome** (the sidebar/topbar styling from the lobby): solid `--panel-bg` console panels with the soft inset top bevel and deep drop shadow instead of borders or gradients; raised clay buttons (135deg `--clay-top`→`--clay-base`, gradient flips to 315deg on hover, sinks with inset shadow + scale .96 when pressed; red clay for primary CTAs); recessed milled wells (`--milled-dark` + inset shadow) for readouts, inputs and grouped controls, with the selected segment raised back out of its well; carbon-fiber weave on the page backdrop only, never on panels. Copy the tokens and shadow values from `index-v2.html` (grep `--clay-top`, `.console-panel`) rather than inventing new ones. No blue left-edge accent lines on rows (reads as an AI tell).
